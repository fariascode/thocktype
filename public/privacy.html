thocktype · privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Without an account

Everything stays in your browser: your tests, settings, tags and key stats. Nothing is sent to our servers.

With an account

If you sign in, we keep in our database (Supabase) what's needed to sync your devices:

  • Your email, to identify you and send you sign-in, confirmation or password links.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google name, email and profile photo. We don't access anything else in your Google account.
  • Your results: speed, accuracy, mode, language, tags and date of each test, and your daily challenge attempts.
  • Key stats: how often you hit or missed each key, for the coach.
  • Your settings and setting presets, and your interface language (so emails reach you in your language).
  • Your profile, if you create one: username, name, photo and bio.

What other people see

If your profile is public, anyone can see your username, name, photo, bio, results, and your place in the daily challenge ranking and the global leaderboard. If you make it private, nobody else sees your results and you don't appear on leaderboards. Your email is never shown.

If you send a duel, the link carries your result, a recording of your keystrokes and your profile name: whoever opens it can see them.

Services we use

  • Supabase: accounts and database. Vercel: app hosting. Resend: sending account emails.
  • Wikipedia: in wiki mode, your browser asks Wikipedia directly for a random article.
  • Google Fonts: optional fonts, except Cascadia Code, are downloaded from Google only if you pick them.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • No ads, no trackers.

Deleting your data

You can delete local data by clearing this site's data in your browser. To delete your account and everything stored in the cloud, email hola@thocktype.app or ask at github.com/fariascode/thocktype/issues and we'll remove it.
